# Motion trend reference (Nov 2026)

Source: browsing the most popular DaVinci Resolve *Titles* and *Lower Thirds* on Envato Elements.
These are general patterns only; every Motion Resolve design is original.

## Titles
- **Per-character / per-word kinetic presets** — characters rise, wave, scatter or rotate in with a stagger; whole packs are built on this.
- **Typewriter with a hero word** — text types on with a blinking cursor; one word is in an accent colour.
- **AI prompt / search-bar typing** — a rounded input bar, text typing in, a send/arrow icon.
- **Bold condensed stacks** — heavy condensed uppercase, 2–3 stacked lines, tight leading; often a hand-drawn underline or scribble in red.
- **Gradient / glow hero word** — one word in a vivid gradient with soft glow on a dark background.
- **Fast kinetic slams** — words slam in with scale overshoot and motion blur, cut-on-beat.

## Lower thirds
- **Two-tone stacked blocks** — name on one solid block, role on a smaller contrasting block, both wipe in.
- **Slanted / skewed boxes** — parallelogram plates for energy (sport, news, creator).
- **Role chip** — small coloured pill next to or under the name ("Director").
- **Minimal editorial** — name + tracked small caps role, thin rule, no plate.
- **Per-line boxes** — each line gets its own tight highlight box, staggered.

## Numbers
- **Count-up stats** — figure counts from 0 to the value with an ease-out, unit (%/$/+) fixed, label fades after.

## Motion language to adopt
- Stagger everything (60–90 ms between words, 20–35 ms between characters).
- Ease-out entrances (expo/quint), faster ease-in exits (≈60% of entrance time).
- Blur + rise for soft/premium; spring overshoot for social; masked rise for editorial/news.
- Exits mirror the entrance direction or collapse quickly; never all-fade.
